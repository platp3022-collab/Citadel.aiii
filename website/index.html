FAQ

What is BITFISH?

A community memecoin on Solana with a pixel-art fish as its mascot. It has no utility promises, no roadmap of products you must buy — it is a meme, a brand and a community.

Is the liquidity safe?

The token launched on pump.fun, where liquidity is burned once the bonding curve completes and mint authority is revoked. Do not take our word for it — check the contract on Solscan and DEX Screener yourself.

Where can I buy it?

On pump.fun with the contract address from this page, or through any Solana aggregator such as Jupiter. Always paste the CA — never trust a search result.

Are there fake tokens?

Yes — memecoins get cloned within minutes. The only real contract is the one printed on this site. If an address does not match character for character, it is not us.

What is that shark doing in the background?

Eating. The big orange one — spray tan, bleached comb-over, red tie — patrols the page, follows you down as you scroll and swallows any fish that swims into its jaw; the deeper you go, the fewer fish there are to catch. Your pointer is the other one: a shark in a suit, greying at the temples. Catch the big one with it and it bursts into bubbles. Both are cartoons and political parody. Neither is a portrait of a real person, and nobody — least of all anyone they remind you of — is affiliated with this coin or endorses it. You can turn either of them around in 3D from the fish family section.

Are the other fish coins live?

No. BITFISH is the only one trading right now — everything else on the fish family page is a plan. If somebody sells you a "SOLFISH presale" or any other sibling before it is announced here, it is a scam.

Will it go up?

Nobody knows, and anyone who tells you otherwise is lying. Memecoins are extremely volatile and can go to zero. Never put in more than you can lose in the deep.
